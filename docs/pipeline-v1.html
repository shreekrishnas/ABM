ABM Intelligence Pipeline

Thirteen stages take raw account data to evidence-based outreach and a sales handoff. Every fact keeps a status and a source, research stops when it can no longer change a decision, and nothing is drafted until identity and evidence pass the readiness gate.

Loop 1 10 → 6: weak evidence gets one targeted re-research pass Loop 2 7 → 6: a contradiction reopens its question once Refresh watchlist → 5 when the re-check date arrives

Readiness outcomes

Stage 10 gives every selected person exactly one outcome. Checked in this order, so the first rule that matches wins.

Field status

Every important field and fact carries one of six statuses, so uncertainty stays visible all the way to the draft.

Gates

Every check that can stop a record. A blocked item is logged with a reason and goes to a watchlist, the review queue, or suppression, never silently dropped.

Replies and engagement

Each reply is classified and routed. Engagement only counts from people we actually contacted.

Engagement score

Open 10, site visit 30, reply 50, each fading with a 14-day half-life. Handoff at 70, or at once on a positive reply.

0

Below 70: the sequence continues.

LLM gateway

Every model call goes through one gateway that routes the task to a tier and charges the account's budget. Models never decide on their own whether to send.
