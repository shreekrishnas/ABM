# Seller profile: Manch Technologies

This brief records what the platform knows about Manch Technologies (the seller) and how that
knowledge drives fit scoring, research, buying-group mapping and drafts. The encoded profile is
`src/lib/seller/manch.ts`; you can view it in the app under **Settings → Seller profile**.

> Research was done from public sources on 2026-10-06. manchtech.com itself could not be fetched
> from the build environment (network policy), so facts come from search snippets of manchtech.com
> pages, review sites and press. **Please have the Manch team confirm or correct anything below.**

## 1. Who Manch is

- Bengaluru, India; founded 2017 by Suresh Anantpurkar (CEO) and A. V. Asvini Kumar. 11–50 employees.
- Funding: $750k seed (Stellaris Venture Partners, Jan 2019) and a $1.1M pre-Series A (Bharat
  Inclusion Seed Fund, with Pentathlon Ventures). About $1.85M in total.
- **Positioning has evolved.** It started as eSign, eKYC and digital onboarding for Indian
  enterprises and lenders. Today manchtech.com/en presents the **Manch Agentic AI Platform**: agentic
  AI for enterprise process orchestration, built on Master Data Management (MDM), governance and
  integrations. Its comparison content targets Informatica, Reltio, Talend, Boomi, Appian, Power
  Apps, Mendix and OutSystems.

## 2. What Manch sells

| Product / capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| Agentic AI | You describe a process; agents assemble, test and certify, and deploy it, then keep learning. Claims 5–15× faster process capture, configuration and testing. |
| Manch MDM | Customer, vendor, partner, product and material masters; AI duplicate detection; 360° view; a GRC engine for periodic and legacy checks; OCR validation. |
| Process Configurator | Zero-code, drag-and-drop workflows (approvals, contracts, asset tracking, compliance, onboarding) with SLAs and escalations. |
| Digital Onboarding | Partners, distributors, vendors, employees and gig workers, with eKYC, Video KYC, eSign and consent. |
| Verification APIs | PAN, GSTIN, bank account, Aadhaar, driving licence, CIN, mobile, email; 100+ integrations; face match, document classification, anomaly detection. |

**Claimed proof points:** 30M+ transactions, 300+ configurable processes, 100 processes configured by
AI agents, partner onboarding cut from 7 days to 7 hours, go-live in 4–8 weeks, and one client
reaching 100% regulatory compliance with a solution delivered in 3 weeks.

**Customers:** "Fortune 500 companies and unicorns" in India. Public case: **Hindustan Coca-Cola
Beverages** replaced several legacy MDM tools and digitised customer onboarding with real-time API
validation. Another case covers a food-delivery network's partner onboarding. Earlier materials
mention 30+ customers across e-commerce, fintech, FMCG and manufacturing.

**Markets:** India first. It is listed on Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice for the US, UK,
Australia, Germany, Philippines, Saudi Arabia and UAE, which suggests GCC and international
expansion.

## 3. Who is a good fit (how the fit score works)

The score has five components and is scored **on known fields only**, so missing data never counts
as a bad fit:

| Component | Weight | Logic |
|---|---|---|
| Industry | 35 | Primary verticals: FMCG/CPG and beverages, manufacturing, e-commerce and quick commerce, fintech/NBFC/lending. Secondary: pharma distribution, logistics and mobility, retail chains, telecom and energy. |
| Company size | 25 | 1,000+ employees full marks; 500+ strong; 200+ partial; under 200 scores 0. |
| Geography | 15 | India full; GCC, US/UK, SE Asia and Australia partial. |
| Partner network | 15 | How many external parties the business onboards (distributors, vendors, sellers, gig workers), inferred from the vertical. |
| Tech stack | 10 | ERP to integrate with (SAP, Oracle, Dynamics…) +6, legacy MDM to replace (Informatica, Reltio, SAP MDG…) +3, DIY workflow tools (Power Apps, Appian…) +1. |

Tiers: T1 for 80 and above, T2 for 60 and above, otherwise T3. Accounts below 40 stop at stage 3.
Tiers now follow the score, so weekly data changes can move them, unless a person locks a tier by
hand.

## 5. Buying triggers (what research looks for)

ERP or S/4HANA migration; distributor, retail or seller expansion; gig and field-workforce scale-up;
new compliance or audit pressure (GST/e-invoicing, RBI KYC, DPDP); legacy MDM or data-quality
programmes; M&A and ERP consolidation; AI or automation programmes; funding-driven growth.

**Deprioritise when:** a competing MDM programme was signed recently, the company is under ~200
staff with no partner network, there were layoffs or a freeze in the last 90 days, or it has been
acquired or is insolvent.

## 6. Messaging

Drafts lead with a verified fact about the prospect, then use **approved Manch collateral** for the
matching use case: distributor onboarding, vendor onboarding, customer master, gig onboarding,
regulated KYC, or product/material master. Collateral is about Manch, never a claim about the
prospect. The fact guardrail still requires every prospect claim to cite evidence.
